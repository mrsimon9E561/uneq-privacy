uEQ Privacy Policy

Effective October 10, 2026

uEQ is an equalizer for Android that switches EQ settings automatically by song, artist, album, app, place, time and output device. This policy explains what information uEQ uses and where it goes. In short: uEQ has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no servers of its own. Everything it learns stays on your phone.

Information uEQ uses on your phone

  • Now-playing information (song title, artist, album and the playing app), read from media notifications through Android's notification access, so the right EQ can be chosen. uEQ does not read the text of messages or any other notifications' content.
  • Audio output devices (for example the name of connected Bluetooth headphones), so each device can have its own sound.
  • Location, only if you create Place rules, to tell whether you are at a place you saved.
  • Music levels, only if you turn on "Animate faders to music". Android groups this under the Microphone permission, but uEQ never uses the microphone: it reads the loudness of each frequency band of the audio already playing, moment to moment, to draw the animation. No audio is recorded or saved.
  • Your EQs, rules and History (recent songs and their EQ settings, kept for 3 days), stored in the app's private storage.

None of this is sent to the developer or to anyone else. Uninstalling uEQ deletes it.

When uEQ connects to the internet

uEQ only goes online for these features, each started by you:

  • Map for Place rules: map images are loaded from OpenStreetMap (OpenStreetMap privacy policy), which receives the map area you are viewing and your IP address, as with any website.
  • Shared map links: if you share a shortened Google Maps link to uEQ, it follows that link's redirects (without downloading the page) to read the coordinates in it.
  • YouTube search: holding the now-playing line opens a YouTube search for the song in your browser or the YouTube app; that search is then handled by YouTube.
  • Optional Google Drive backup: if you sign in from Settings, uEQ saves a backup of its settings to your own Google Drive, in a file only uEQ can see (the drive.file permission). The developer has no access to it. You can remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Sharing and selling

uEQ does not sell, rent or share your information. It contains no advertising or analytics code.

Backups you make

If you export your EQs to a file, that file goes wherever you choose to save or send it.

Children

uEQ is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
